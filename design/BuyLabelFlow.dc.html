If EasyPost errors (step 6)
Cancel the PaymentIntent (authorization drops off the card), map the error, nothing charged. A timeout is unknown: GET /v2/shipments/{id} — if postage_label exists, continue from step 7; else cancel.
If the DB tx or capture fails (step 8)
Label exists at EasyPost → enqueue refund_orphan (EasyPost refund, retried) and cancel the PaymentIntent; respond 500. Capture failing after a committed label is rare (auth expired) — a worker retries capture, then falls back to a fresh charge with an email.
Batch = this ×N, one charge
Authorize the batch total once, then rows buy in parallel (concurrency 8). Capture only the sum of rows that succeeded (partial capture); the batch reports partial with per-row errors, and the merged PDF comes from EasyPost Batch.label.
