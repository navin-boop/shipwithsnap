The questions everyone asks
Is it really free?
Yes. Carriers pay us a small share of postage for bringing them volume. You pay exactly the discounted rate you see — never a fee, never a plan.
Do I need a label printer?
No. Print on plain paper and tape it on. If you ship every day, a thermal printer pays for itself — we support any 4×6 printer out of the box.
What about my Shopify and Etsy orders?
Connect a store once and open orders appear in Batch. Select them all, buy every label in one click, and tracking numbers post back to the store automatically.
Is the postage the same as at the counter?
Same carriers, same services, same delivery. The only thing that changes is the price.
